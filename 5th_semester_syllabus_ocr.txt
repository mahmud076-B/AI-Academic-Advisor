Credit: 3.0
Pre-requisite; MAT 1141 (Differential and Integral Calculus), MAT 2241
(Linear Algebra and Complex Variablew)
Course Content:
An Introduction: Definition of Statistics, Uses and importance, Variables,
Measurement level of variables, Sample and Population,
Measures of central tendency: Introduction, the arithmetic mean,

’ Geometric mean, Harmonic mean, Median, mode, computing of means for
grouped data and ungrouped data, Advantages, and disadvantages, and Uses
of all measures.
Measures of dispersion: Introduction of dispersion, Absolute and Relative
measures, Range, mean deviation, Standard deviation, Variance, Quartile
