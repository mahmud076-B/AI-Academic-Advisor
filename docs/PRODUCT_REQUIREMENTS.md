## MVP Scope

The MVP will focus on:

- Allowing students to authenticate.
- Allowing students to create or complete an academic profile.
- Storing academic context such as department, batch, section, semester, courses, and class routine.
- Providing an AI chat experience.
- Keeping chat history.
- Allowing students to share campus experiences.
- Retrieving relevant Campus Memory entries for AI context.
- Providing personalized academic assistance.
